I took the street that curves away from home before returning to it. Nothing important happened.

A window was open above a closed shop. Rain remained in the cracks between paving stones. Someone had left a book on a low wall, protected by a clear plastic bag.

The extra distance made the familiar feel briefly unclaimed.
