There is a particular relief in removing one thing from a crowded surface. The room does not become empty. It becomes legible.

This space begins the same way: few parts, no audience metrics, and no obligation to make noise. Just enough structure to hold a thought.

## A useful constraint

Keeping less asks a better question than adding more: *does this belong here?*

The answer can change. That is part of the practice.
