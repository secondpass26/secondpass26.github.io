This will be my routine from Monday to Friday, at least until I start work. The main goal is to cut back on social media and doomscrolling. I will reduce or even stop watching YouTube videos while I work, which is something I usually do.

I think this might be tough for the first few days or weeks. But I need to trust that it will eventually get easier and that I will be able to focus for longer. Work might be more fun too.

| Time | Routine |
| --- | --- |
| 7:30 a.m. |   Wake up. It might be even earlier, depending on when my son wakes up. Have some coffee and water, then go for a swim, run, or gym session. |
| 8:30 a.m. |   Return home, wake up my daughter, and have breakfast with her. No videos or internet at this point, but reading books is okay. |
| 9:15 a.m. |   Take my daughter to school. |
| 9:30 a.m. |   Return home and have a quick shower. |
| 10:00 a.m. |  Start my first two-hour deep work session. |
| 12:00 p.m. |  Have lunch. |
| 1:30 p.m. |   Walk with my son. |
| 3:00 p.m. |   Start my second deep work session, aiming for at least two hours. |
| 6:00 p.m. |   Fetch my daughter. |
| 6:00pm to 11:00 p.m. |     Have dinner and spend time with the kids. |
| 11:00 p.m. |  Relax and watch YouTube. |


I will not surf the internet or check social media aimlessly. There is no need to know the news, or anything else, immediately. The key is to keep my brain fresh for deep work. I need to embrace boredom. That is the only way I can move ahead.

I will allow myself to check social media on weekends, perhaps for 15 minutes each day.
